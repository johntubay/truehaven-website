A typical bathroom

What two weeks with us looks like.

A typical bathroom remodel, stage by stage, with the walkthrough you get along the way. Drag or use the arrows to follow along.

Day 1

Protect and remove

We cover floors and walkways, then take the old bathroom out down to the studs.

We show you what we found behind the walls.
Days 2 to 3

Plumbing and framing

Drains, valves, and wiring get set for the new layout, lighting, and fixtures.

We confirm fixture spots with you before walls close.
Days 4 to 5

Shower pan and waterproofing

New walls go up and the shower area is fully waterproofed and sealed.

You see the waterproofing before tile covers it.
Days 6 to 8

Tile

Floor tile and shower walls are set, then grouted and sealed.

This is when the room starts to look new.
Days 9 to 11

Tub, vanity, and fixtures

Glass, vanity, faucets, toilet, and lighting are installed.

We test every fixture with you in the room.
Days 12 to 13

Paint and details

Paint, trim, mirror, and accessories, followed by a deep clean.

We make a short list of anything to adjust.
Day 14

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished bathroom together and handle every touch-up on the list.

Done when you say it’s done.

    How it works

    No guesswork, from the first visit to the last walkthrough.

    The two questions we hear most are “how long?” and “how much?” Our process answers both before any work starts.

    1. In-home consultation

      We visit, listen, measure, and talk through what you want. We never push materials or upgrades you didn’t ask for.

    2. Estimate in 1 to 2 days

      Your written estimate arrives by text or email, and we follow up so every question gets answered.

    3. Reserve your spot

      A $1,000 deposit holds your place on the schedule. We tell you which project you follow and confirm your start date.

    4. Daily walkthroughs

      We are on site every working day. If you live in the home, we walk you through the progress each afternoon.

    5. Final touches

      We go over the finished room together and take care of anything on your list.

    How long does a bathroom remodel take?

    An average bathroom takes about two weeks. Smaller, simpler bathrooms can go faster, and larger layouts or special-order materials take longer. You get your schedule before we start.

    How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

    It depends on the size of the room, the layout, and the materials you choose. After the in-home consultation, we send a written estimate within one to two days, so you know the cost before you commit.

    What happens if you find a problem behind the walls?

    Older homes sometimes hide water damage or outdated plumbing. If we find something, we show you the same day, explain your options and the cost, and wait for your OK before changing anything.

    Will someone be at my home every day?

    Yes. Our crew is on site every working day from start to finish. If you live in the home during the project, we walk you through the day’s progress each afternoon.
