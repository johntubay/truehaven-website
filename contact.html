How long does a bathroom remodel take?

An average bathroom takes about two weeks. Smaller, simpler bathrooms can go faster, and larger layouts or special-order materials take longer. You get your schedule before we start.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

It depends on the size of the room, the layout, and the materials you choose. After the in-home consultation, we send a written estimate within one to two days, so you know the cost before you commit.

What happens if you find a problem behind the walls?

Older homes sometimes hide water damage or outdated plumbing. If we find something, we show you the same day, explain your options and the cost, and wait for your OK before changing anything.

Will someone be at my home every day?

Yes. Our crew is on site every working day from start to finish. If you live in the home during the project, we walk you through the day’s progress each afternoon.
